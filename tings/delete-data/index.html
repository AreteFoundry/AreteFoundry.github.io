Delete your Tings data

Tings is a local-first planner: your habits, tasks, logs, notes, places and settings live only on your device. There is no Tings account and no server-side copy of your data.

Last updated: October 5, 2026 · Applies to the Tings app by Arete Foundry

How to delete your data

  1. (Optional) Export a backup first In Tings, open Settings → backup to export a copy you can keep or re-import later. Skip this if you want everything gone.
  2. Delete the app's data on your device Installed app: open your device's Settings → Apps → Tings → Storage → Clear data (or Clear storage). Web app: open your browser's site-settings for the Tings site and clear its site data.
  3. …or simply uninstall the app Uninstalling Tings removes the app together with all of its data.
That's the whole process. Because your planner is stored only on your device, clearing the app's data or uninstalling deletes everything — no request, waiting period, or account cancellation needed.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted — all of it

  • Habits, tasks, schedules and plans
  • Completion logs and history
  • Notes on your items
  • Saved places and addresses
  • Reminder and alarm choices
  • All app settings and preferences

Kept — nothing

  • No account data — there are no Tings accounts
  • No server-side copy — Arete Foundry does not operate a server that stores your planner
  • No analytics, crash reports, or tracking data
  • No retention period — after deletion, nothing remains with us

Two small notes: backups you exported yourself are ordinary files on your device — delete them like any file. If you used the optional encrypted sharing in the web app, revoke it from the item in Settings; the encrypted relay copies are unreadable to us and age out of the relay. Some features (weather, address search, driving estimates) send a one-time request to an outside service when you use them; those requests are not retained by Tings.

Request deletion or ask a question

You do not need to ask us to delete your data — the steps above remove everything instantly. If you would still like to send a deletion request or ask about your data, use the feedback form (also reachable from About → send feedback inside the app). We will confirm that no data is retained.
